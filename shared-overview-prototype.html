Variant A — Onboarding journey

Lao Post: system overview

A cross-channel parcel service that lets customers create shipments, staff manage fulfillment, and riders complete delivery.

1. What the system does

Customers

Create and track parcel deliveries through supported customer channels.

Operations

Review shipments, coordinate fulfillment, and resolve delivery exceptions.

Riders

Accept assigned work and report observable delivery outcomes.

2. System at a glance

Customer/Admin clients→API service→Queue + workers→Delivery integrations

Clients use authenticated HTTPS contracts. Long-running work crosses a queue boundary. Shared business identifiers connect user-visible shipment state across components.

  1. Create a shipment — customer request becomes a trackable shipment. Read workflow
  2. Rider delivery — an assigned parcel moves through pickup, attempt, and outcome. Read workflow

It coordinates parcel creation, fulfillment, delivery, and tracking across customer, staff, and rider experiences.

Who interacts with it?

Customers initiate and track delivery; operations staff manage exceptions; riders perform fulfillment; external carriers exchange delivery events.
